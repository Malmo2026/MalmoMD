Important safety information: Prescription ED medications are not for everyone. Do not use ED medications if you take nitrates (for chest pain) or guanylate cyclase stimulators (such as riociguat), as this can cause a dangerous drop in blood pressure. Tell your provider about all medications and supplements you take, and any medical conditions—especially heart problems, low blood pressure, recent heart attack or stroke, kidney or liver disease, or eye conditions.

Seek emergency care for an erection lasting more than 4 hours (priapism), sudden vision loss, sudden hearing loss, chest pain, or severe dizziness. Common side effects may include headache, flushing, indigestion, nasal congestion, back pain, and dizziness.
