How much does the program cost?

Will this work for me?

Our providers will explore prescribing compounded GLP-1 medications for you, if appropriate - with effective, GLP-1 ingredients. Compounded medication can provide incredible results for you, using active GLP-1 ingredients that help you reach your goals.

What if my insurance doesn't cover the medication?

While all MEDVi prescriptions are cash-pay, your insurance may reimburse you for branded options. We keep compounded medications fairly priced so no insurance is necessary while still working toward your goal weight.

What can I expect after I sign up?

After you complete your online visit, our medical providers will review your answers and determine whether treatment may be right for you. If appropriate, you will be prescribed medication which will be shipped right from the pharmacy to your door.
