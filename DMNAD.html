Medications prescribed through Malmo Health are dispensed by U.S.-based 503B compounding pharmacies. These pharmacies prepare medications individually in response to a prescription from a licensed healthcare provider and must follow applicable federal and state compounding regulations, as well as professional standards such as USP 795 and USP 797.

Compounded medications are prepared for individual patients when commercially available alternatives are not suitable. They are not FDA-approved, but 503B compounding pharmacies are licensed by state pharmacy boards and operate under federal compounding laws.
